# FarmBot — Creative Direction

**Status: this is the bot's spec of record (2026-09-08).** FarmBot's prior content (paths,
look register, seed pools) was fully discarded and rebuilt from this document — see
`FARMBOT_PATH_BUILD_STATE.md` for build status and the rebuild plan history. Any future work
on FarmBot's content should be checked against this doc, not against what previously existed.

Written by Kevin, verbatim.

---

## The Big Idea

A **cute, idyllic countryside universe** inspired by cozy farming games like Hay Day and the
warmth of cozy anime / slice-of-life.

The world should feel like a place where **everything is beautiful, friendly, peaceful, and
just a little bit magical**. Farm life is not portrayed as hard labor or realistic agriculture.
Instead, everyday activities become charming little moments: picking strawberries, feeding
ducklings, baking bread, gathering eggs, selling vegetables at the village market, sitting
beside a pond, or watching the sunset with a flock of animals nearby.

**Hay Day provides the world and variety. Cozy anime provides the characters, emotion,
atmosphere, and visual storytelling.**

The ultimate feeling:

> **"I want to live there."**

---

## 1. LOOK & FEEL

### Core aesthetic

Cozy anime; whimsical storybook countryside; charming farming-game world; soft, colorful,
painterly environments; warm and inviting; wholesome; peaceful; playful; nostalgic; slightly
dreamy; idyllic; abundant; handcrafted.

The imagery should feel **beautifully imperfect in a charming way**, but never dirty, bleak,
harsh, or realistic.

Think: **cozy anime + Hay Day + cottagecore + storybook illustration + gentle fantasy**

### The most important visual rule

**Cute > cozy > whimsical > beautiful > realistic.**

Realism should never override the cute, idealized world.

---

## 2. THE WORLD

The bot should generate an entire **cozy countryside universe**, rather than simply generating
farms.

The farm is the home base, but characters can wander into: farmhouses, barns, gardens,
orchards, pastures, flower fields, forests, lakes, rivers, waterfalls, village streets,
markets, bakeries, cafés, flower shops, craft shops, inns, fishing docks, village squares,
festival grounds.

The world should feel **densely populated with charming little details**: flower-covered
fences, wooden signs, baskets, barrels, lanterns, garden paths, market stalls, birdhouses,
bridges, flower boxes, hay bales, chickens wandering through the road, cats sleeping on
windowsills, etc.

---

## 3. CHARACTERS

Humans should be **cute, friendly, approachable anime villagers**. There are no stern, rugged,
intimidating farmers.

### Character qualities

Warm expressions; gentle smiles; friendly eyes; soft facial features; relaxed poses; playful
body language; wholesome clothing; charming personalities.

### Character archetypes

Cheerful farm girl; cozy farm boy; gardener; baker; shepherd; herbalist; village shopkeeper;
flower seller; café owner; innkeeper; artisan; weaver; potter; carpenter; fisher; horse keeper;
friendly village grandma; friendly village grandpa; traveling adventurer who settled down.

Characters should frequently **interact with animals**, rather than simply standing beside them.

---

## 4. ANIMALS — A MAJOR SIGNATURE

Animals should be **abundant**. The bot should err toward *more cute animals*, not fewer.

### Farm animals

Cows, calves, sheep, lambs, goats, baby goats, pigs, piglets, horses, ponies, chickens, chicks,
roosters, ducks, ducklings, geese, rabbits, bunnies.

### Companion animals

Farm dogs, puppies, farm cats, kittens.

### Baby-animal bias

Baby animals should receive particularly high weighting. Chicks, ducklings, lambs, piglets,
calves, baby goats, foals, puppies, kittens, and baby rabbits are all highly desirable.

### Animal personalities

Curious, playful, sleepy, mischievous, affectionate, shy, hungry, excited, gentle.

### Animal behaviors

Following the character; nuzzling the character; sitting beside the character; sleeping in
hay; eating from a trough; being held; being brushed; being fed; playing together; chasing
butterflies; peeking through windows; wandering through gardens; following paths; gathering
around feeding time; sleeping under trees; playing in the meadow.

### Animal density

The generator can vary animal density: **Low** (1-2), **Medium** (3-5), **High** (6-10),
**Adorable chaos** (an entire menagerie).

The occasional scene should basically be: **"Why are there twelve animals following this
girl?"** Because it's adorable.

---

## 5. FARM ACTIVITIES

Activities should be **gentle, satisfying, and charming**, rather than labor-intensive.

### Gardening

Planting seeds, watering flowers, watering vegetables, picking strawberries, harvesting
carrots, picking tomatoes, gathering herbs, picking pumpkins, harvesting wheat, picking apples,
gathering berries, collecting mushrooms, arranging flowers.

### Animal care

Gathering eggs, feeding chickens, milking cows, feeding lambs, brushing horses, grooming
ponies, feeding piglets, holding chicks, bottle-feeding baby animals, bathing puppies, playing
with kittens.

### Homestead chores

Hanging laundry, sweeping the porch, gathering firewood, decorating the farmhouse, arranging
flowers, organizing baskets, preparing a picnic, bringing in the harvest, filling bird feeders.

The emphasis is on **the cozy moment**, not the work itself.

---

## 6. FOOD & BAKING

Food should be visually abundant and ridiculously inviting.

### Ingredients

Strawberries, apples, peaches, cherries, blueberries, raspberries, carrots, tomatoes,
potatoes, corn, wheat, pumpkins, herbs, mushrooms, honey, fresh milk, eggs.

### Homemade food

Fresh bread, apple pie, berry pie, pumpkin pie, pancakes, waffles, jam, honey, cheese, butter,
omelets, vegetable soup, stew, pasta, cookies, cakes, fruit tarts, tea, lemonade, hot cocoa.

Food should communicate **warmth, abundance, and home**.

---

## 7. CRAFTING & ARTISAN LIFE

Everything is handmade and charming.

Baking, jam making, cheese making, butter churning, honey harvesting, candle making, soap
making, pottery, basket weaving, knitting, sewing, wood carving, flower arranging, wreath
making, herbal tea making, preserving fruit.

### Buildings

Bakery, dairy cottage, jam kitchen, pottery shop, weaver's cottage, carpenter's workshop,
flower shop, candle shop, general store.

No industrial production.

---

## 8. VILLAGE & MARKET LIFE

The village should feel like a **living cozy game world**.

Scenarios include: village market day, selling vegetables, selling eggs, selling homemade jam,
buying fresh bread, visiting the bakery, visiting the flower shop, browsing market stalls,
carrying produce through town, delivering a basket of baked goods, sitting at the village café,
talking with neighbors, reading the village notice board, picking up a parcel, visiting the
village square, shopping for handmade goods.

The market should be colorful, bustling, but **never stressful**.

---

## 9. LEISURE & "NOTHING HAPPENING"

A critical part of the bot. Not every image needs an activity.

Scenes can simply be: sitting on the porch, drinking tea, reading beneath a tree, watching
rain, napping in the hay, watching the sunset, stargazing, picking wildflowers, sitting beside
a stream, feeding ducks, walking the dog, watching fireflies, sharing a picnic, resting after
a pleasant day.

The bot should capture **the joy of having nowhere to be**.

---

## 10. SEASONS

### Spring
Cherry blossoms, baby animals, seedlings, rain, wildflowers, fresh green fields, spring markets.

### Summer
Sunflowers, strawberries, tall grass, warm sunlight, picnics, fireflies, lakeside scenes,
berry harvest.

### Autumn
Pumpkins, apples, falling leaves, harvest baskets, warm sweaters, hay, harvest festivals,
cozy kitchens.

### Winter
Snow-covered farms, snowy animals, fireplaces, hot cocoa, baking, winter markets, lanterns,
snowman building, warm farmhouse interiors.

---

## 11. WEATHER & ATMOSPHERE

Use weather as an emotional modifier: warm morning sunlight, golden afternoon, sunset, blue
hour, starry night, gentle rain, misty morning, foggy countryside, fresh after rain, soft
snowfall, summer haze, crisp autumn afternoon.

Atmospheric details: fireflies, sunbeams, floating pollen, falling petals, falling leaves,
snowflakes, steam from cooking, smoke from chimneys, dew-covered flowers, rain droplets,
golden dust motes.

---

## 12. FESTIVALS & SPECIAL EVENTS

The world should feel like it has a calendar.

### Spring
Flower festival, cherry blossom picnic, seedling festival, egg hunt, spring market.

### Summer
Strawberry festival, sunflower festival, summer picnic, firefly evening, lakeside festival,
berry harvest.

### Autumn
Harvest festival, pumpkin festival, apple harvest, corn maze, pie contest, lantern festival,
autumn market, bonfire evening.

### Winter
First snowfall, winter market, gingerbread baking, snowman building, tree decorating, lantern
walk, cozy winter gathering.

---

## 13. DECORATION / WORLD DETAIL POOLS

These small details are extremely important.

Flower-covered fences, wooden signs, lanterns, flower boxes, wicker baskets, hay bales, wooden
barrels, produce crates, birdhouses, wind chimes, scarecrows, stone paths, cobblestone
streets, wooden bridges, decorative wagons, flower arches, hanging baskets, bunting, picnic
blankets, butterflies, bees, songbirds, wildflowers.

The goal is **high environmental richness without visual clutter**.

---

## 14. GENTLE MAGIC

A small, low-weight pool. Not epic fantasy. Just subtle enchantment: glowing fireflies,
magical mushrooms, slightly oversized flowers, sparkling ponds, enchanted forests, tiny
friendly forest spirits, glowing lanterns, moonlit gardens, magical herb gardens.

The world can occasionally feel like a fairytale without becoming a fantasy-adventure bot.

---

## 15. CAMERA / COMPOSITION

A dedicated composition pool prevents every image from becoming the same character portrait.

Wide countryside establishing shot; character within a large landscape; medium character
scene; close-up; over-the-shoulder; looking through a farmhouse window; barn doorway framing;
foreground foliage; cozy interior composition; village street perspective; market scene;
low-angle scenic view; high-angle village view.

Some images should make the **world** the star rather than the character.

---

## 16. THE GENERATION MODEL

Rather than randomly selecting one item from every pool, build scenes in layers:

### Layer 1 — SCENE ARCHETYPE

Examples: rainy farmhouse morning, autumn village market, summer evening by the pond, spring
planting day, cozy bakery afternoon, animal feeding time, first snowfall, harvest festival,
picnic in the meadow, quiet sunset on the porch.

### Layer 2 — SCENE DNA

Select compatible: location + activity + animals + season + weather + food + props +
character + atmosphere.

### Layer 3 — SERENDIPITY

Add 2-4 unexpected cute details. Examples: kitten sleeping in a strawberry basket, ducklings
following the character, goat poking its head through a window, puppy carrying a tiny stick,
chickens wandering through the bakery, lamb sleeping beside a picnic basket, cat riding on a
sleepy cow.

This creates **coherent randomness** rather than random objects thrown into a scene.

---

## 17. HARD EXCLUSIONS

The world should actively avoid: tractors, combines, industrial machinery, modern agricultural
equipment, modern vehicles, industrial farms, factory buildings, realistic commercial
agriculture, harsh manual labor, dirty or gritty environments, serious-looking farmers,
intimidating characters, aggressive animals, bleak landscapes, dystopian imagery, dark/gritty
fantasy, modern urban elements, anything that makes the world feel stressful or unpleasant.

**Implementation note (added, not part of Kevin's original doc):** this list must be encoded
as what the world POSITIVELY IS, never as banned nouns in any prompt-facing text — this bot
has a proven, documented failure mode where a banned noun leaks into the render anyway because
Flux/CLIP conditioning doesn't process negation (see `shared-blocks.js`'s `FARMBOT_COZY_NEUTRAL`
header comment for the original incident).

---

## THE NORTH STAR

Every generation should answer **yes** to these questions:

Is it cute? Is it cozy? Is it inviting? Are the characters approachable? Are the animals
adorable? Does the world feel abundant and cared for? Would I want to live there?

If the answer to those is yes, the bot is doing its job.

The ultimate target isn't **"a cute farming scene."**

It's:

> **A perfect little countryside life, captured one adorable moment at a time.**
