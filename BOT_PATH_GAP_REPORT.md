# Bot Path Gap Report — proposed new paths per bot (2026-07-04)

Full-fleet audit: playbook re-read in full + every active bot's module mapped (per-path scene identity,
hero type, axes, locks, clusters) via 16 parallel read-only agents. DragonBot excluded per Kevin.
MechBot (decommissioned), RetroBot (parked for Palm Royale), OutlawBot (in dev, unscheduled) excluded
as inactive. Proposals are gap-driven: each names the hole it fills, the PROVEN shape to clone, and its
signature money-shot axis. All follow the standard build law: MVP-25 pools → render-test → Kevin verdict
→ scale. Tier 1 = biggest vibe-pusher / clearest gap. Tier 2 = strong variety add.

**Fleet-level white space that shows up across bots** (the recurring gaps this audit found):
1. **WINTER is almost unowned.** EarthBot has no snow path, FaeBot is perpetual summer, TinyBot's
   village family has no winter member, MangaBot has no snow register. Multiple winter proposals below.
2. **RAIN as a hero register** — only ChibiBot plays in rain. MangaBot (Garden-of-Words) and BloomBot
   (drenched blooms) both want it.
3. **NIGHT MARKETS / festivals-at-night** — lantern-lit commerce scenes are a proven crowd-pleaser shape
   (twilight-village, japanese-festival) that FaeBot, TinyBot, and ChibiBot can each own in their register.
4. **Water verticals** — BloomBot has no water-flowers, SteamBot has no undersea, OceanBot has no kelp
   forest or multi-species feeding event.
5. **Interior-as-hero is under-exploited** — hidden-conservatory / the-sanctum / arcane-library prove the
   shape; PixelBot, SteamBot, GothBot all have high-value interiors to add.

## BloomBot (19 paths — flowers always the hero)

Covered today: grand vistas, architecture+blooms, surreal flower-worlds, macro/arrangements, pollinator
co-heroes. Gaps: **no water, no rain, no night, no winter, no agricultural mega-fields.** All current
paths are fair-weather daylight/sunset.

- **T1 `water-garden`** — lotus/lily ponds, floating flower rafts, mirror-still dark water DOUBLING the
  bloom color (the conservatory water_feature lesson says reflection doubles color — here it's the whole
  premise). Koi/dragonfly accent gated ~30%. Clone the scene-as-hero shape; money-shot axis =
  `reflection` (what the water mirrors: blooms / sky / both).
- **T1 `flower-fields`** — the cultivated mega-field: Dutch tulip stripes, Provence lavender rows,
  sunflower seas, poppy hills, aerial + ground-level vantages. Iconic imagery zero paths touch; distinct
  from `landscape` (wild carpet on landform) by GEOMETRY — rows, stripes, waves of a single crop.
  Money-shot axis = `field_geometry`.
- **T2 `moon-garden`** — moonlit night garden: white/silver blooms (moonflower, night jasmine, white
  wisteria), full moon, fireflies, cool-silver palette. The bot's first night register; sibling of
  sunset-flowers with the light inverted. Money-shot = `moonlight_effect` (silvered petals / glow-edge).
- **T2 `rain-garden`** — storm-light drenched blooms: saturated wet color, droplets on petals, moody
  sky, petals scattered on wet stone. Money-shot = `rain_state` (drizzle / downpour / just-after).
- **Spectacle candidate: `great-blossom-tree`** — ONE colossal ancient flowering tree (wisteria/magnolia/
  sakura-giant) as monumental hero, jack-and-the-giant-flower energy but a TREE canopy world.

## BrickBot (14 paths — LEGO MOC photography)

Covered: themed narrative worlds, nature-translated-to-brick, islands, showcase, mech, theme-park.
Gaps: **the biggest real LEGO themes are missing — City, Trains, Haunted, Modular street.** Bot is also
tonally all-sunny.

- **T1 `lego-city`** — modern LEGO City life: fire station mid-callout, construction site, busy modular
  downtown street, harbor docks, police chase. THE core LEGO theme and the AFOL modular-street icon,
  totally uncovered. Clone the western/fantasy axis stack (scene_type / minifig_action / build_technique /
  register ~85% City-heritage / camera MANDATORY). Money-shot = the modular-street facade row.
- **T1 `lego-trains`** — LEGO trains winding through all-brick layouts: steam engines on studded viaducts,
  cargo yards, mountain tunnels, crossing gates. Iconic theme; differentiates cleanly from ToyBot's
  model-train (that's HO-scale hobbyist realism; this is unmistakably brick-built world + brick train).
  Money-shot axis = `trackwork` (viaduct / spiral / drawbridge / yard).
- **T2 `haunted-brick`** — LEGO Creator Haunted-House register: spooky-FUN brick manor, glow-ghost
  minifigs, jack-o-lantern stalls, full-moon bats — Halloween tone the bot completely lacks (stays
  cute-spooky, never horror). 50%-gated `apparition` layer.
- **T2 `micro-skyline`** — microscale architecture: whole city skylines/famous skylines at micro-build
  scale, no minifigs at all (architecture-set register). New scale language for the bot.

## ChibiBot (25 paths — cute critters, no humans)

Covered: creature close-ups, pairs, 7 real-world outings, cozy/rain interiors, 6 villages.
Gaps: **no autumn anywhere in the outing family, no night-festival outing, no school/learning scenes,
village family has no sky biome.**

- **T1 `creature-autumn-day`** — outing-family clone (cheapest possible win — shared archetype + 4 bespoke
  pools): leaf-pile jumping, pumpkin patch, apple picking, acorn gathering, sweater weather. Completes the
  season set (beach=summer, snow-day=winter, autumn=MISSING).
- **T1 `creature-lantern-festival`** — outing at a NIGHT lantern festival: paper lanterns, festival games,
  sparklers, floating sky-lanterns overhead. Twilight-village proves the palette sells; this adds the
  BAND-OF-FRIENDS activity layer. Money-shot = `lantern_spectacle` (sky-lantern release / lantern canal).
- **T2 `sky-village`** — 7th village: cloud-kingdom biome (cottages on cloud-tufts, rainbow bridges,
  balloon docks, star-lamp posts). Village archetype is fully proven; one new biome + resident tag filter.
- **T2 `creature-school`** — tiny critter schoolhouse day: art class, show-and-tell, recess, naptime mats.
  Interior + group register between cozy-interior (solo) and outings (outdoor).

## DinoBot (13 paths — photoreal Mesozoic)

Covered: portraits/action, full social spectrum, swamp+ocean, aerial, set-pieces. Gaps: **zero night
paths, zero storm/weather-drama paths, zero cold-climate paths.** The whole feed is warm daylight.

- **T1 `dino-nights`** — nocturnal Mesozoic: moonlit watering holes, hunting by starlight, eyeshadow
  glints, fireflies over fern-plains, Milky Way over sauropod silhouettes (real night sky per EarthBot's
  night-landscapes rules). Money-shot = `night_light` (moon phase / starfield / pre-dawn).
- **T1 `storm-season`** — dinosaurs in DRAMATIC weather: torrential rain sheeting off a T-rex, lightning
  over a fleeing herd, monsoon-flooded plains, dust-storm walls. Jurassic-Park-in-the-rain is THE iconic
  register and nothing rolls it reliably (phenomenon gates are 20%). Keep no-gore/grounded rules.
- **T2 `polar-dinos`** — REAL paleo-accuracy win: polar dinosaurs (Alaskan/Antarctic fauna — Nanuqsaurus,
  Leaellynasaura), snow-dusted feathers, aurora skies, ice-edge coasts. Entirely new biome + palette.
- **Revival candidate:** `volcanic-apocalypse` sits dormant on disk — rebuild lean on the axis system
  (the steampunk-hybrid revival law: rebuild on the current engine, don't restore old blocks).
- **Kevin-call flagged, not proposed:** Cenozoic megafauna (mammoths/smilodon) — visually fresh but
  arguably off-brand for a DINObot. Decide the brand line before anyone builds it.

## DreamBot (31 paths — dream-world wildcard)

Covered: bubble-bot family, candy vistas, botanical/conservatory/butterflies, fine-art surreal (the two
WIP paths), pulp comedy. As the wildcard bot, gaps = high-concept aesthetics nobody owns:

- **T1 `pocket-planets`** — Little-Prince planetoids: tiny round mini-worlds floating in soft pastel
  space, each self-contained (one cottage + one tree + a ring of ducks; a lighthouse planet; a garden
  planet with its own tiny moon). Iconic beloved aesthetic, zero fleet coverage. Money-shot =
  `planet_theme` × `orbit_companion` (what circles it).
- **T1 `dream-express`** — a whimsical train traveling through impossible dreamscapes: sky rails over
  cloud seas, star-bridge crossings, aurora tunnels, lantern-lit carriages (Spirited-Away sea-train
  register). Journey shape (tiny-vehicles law: verb-led beats + layered world + the train off-center).
- **T2 `cloud-harbor`** — ships sailing seas of cloud: lighthouse beacons on cloud reefs, sky-whales
  optional-gated, sunset cloud-surf. Lush painterly sibling to dream-logic's austere surrealism.
- **T2 `dreamscape-nocturne`** — proven two-sibling A/B move (far-eden law): the dreamscape shape with
  the light inverted — moonlit candy-worlds, lantern-lit streams, luminous flora. Shares pools, new
  medium + light axes only.

## EarthBot (26 paths — real Earth, strict photo rules)

Covered: global + 7 regional vistas, forests, light/season/geology/night, 8-beach family. Gaps:
**no dedicated winter/ice path, no North-America-wild-north regional, storms were dropped and never
rebuilt.**

- **T1 `winter-wonder`** — deep-winter landscapes as their own path (not a seasonal-shift roll): hoarfrost
  forests, frozen waterfalls, rime-crusted pines, blue ice caves, snow-laden peaks at alpenglow. The
  feed reads warm year-round; this is a whole missing palette. Regional-formula axes + `ice_feature`
  money-shot.
- **T1 `north-wild`** — the missing regional: Alaska/Yukon/Canadian Rockies (Moraine/Banff turquoise
  lakes, Denali, boreal taiga, tundra autumn reds, aurora nights — aurora precedent exists via
  iceland-raw). Clone the proven regional 6-axis shape + biome-agnostic prefix
  (`North American raw nature, photograph`).
- **T2 `storm-earth`** — supercells, lightning barrages, monsoon walls, mammatus skies over plains.
  NOTE: `dramatic-sky`/`weather-drama` were dropped historically — dig out WHY before building (if it
  was a quality kill, the axis system + wow-medium may have changed the math; if it was a taste kill,
  skip).

## FaeBot (19 paths — painted enchanted forest)

Covered: mythic portraits, druid family, 5 villages, city/vista, crowd (fairy-swarm). Gaps: **perpetual
summer (no winter court), no market/commerce scene, no magical-ANIMAL hero path.**

- **T1 `goblin-market`** — THE iconic fae scene nothing renders: a night market of fae merchants —
  lantern-lit stalls of potions/trinkets/bottled starlight, odd little merchants, critter customers.
  Crowd machinery proven by fairy-swarm (≥6 figures, verb-led event, plural prefix). Money-shot =
  `market_wares` (the one impossible thing for sale).
- **T1 `frost-court`** — winter fae: ice-crystal fae in silver-birch winter forest, frozen-lace wings,
  frost-flower gardens, aurora through bare branches — ALIVE sparkling winter magic (explicitly not
  GothBot's dead/cursed frost-garden). Fills the season gap on a bot that is 100% green-summer.
- **T2 `spirit-beasts`** — enchanted ANIMALS as heroes: white stag with blossoming antlers, moss-backed
  bear, spirit foxes with ember tails, luminous owls. Pure magical animals (NOT beast-men — respects
  Kevin's no-animal-hybrid rule, which banned fusing animals onto the elder, not animal heroes).

## GothBot (22 paths — hauntingly beautiful dark)

Covered: vistas, architecture/interiors, goth women, hunters, prince, 2 creatures. Gaps: **no event/crowd

- **T1 `masquerade-ball`** — grand gothic masquerade: candlelit ballroom, vampires in ornate masks and

- **T2 `dark-familiars`** — gothic creatures at beauty-first intimacy: a murder of crows on cathedral
  gargoyles, a raven with jewel eyes on a tombstone, black cats in a witch's window, white wolves in
  graveyard snow. Fills the creature range between monster-prowl's epic beasts and nothing.
- **T2 `elegy`** — intimate cemetery romance: weeping-angel statuary, a veiled mourner at midnight,
  roses on marble, candle offerings. Romantic-melancholy close register (moonlit-maiden's mood pointed
  at a PLACE).
- **Revival candidate:** `the-haunting` (wraith/spectre) is parked mid-experiment — the ghost register
  is otherwise absent from the roster.
- **Quality lever (not a path):** the look pool has only 3 looks, all inked/anime — the painterly-oil and
  hyperreal registers the bot used to render are now UNREACHABLE. Expanding
  `gothbot_look_register.json` to ~6-8 looks is probably worth more than any single new path.

## MangaBot (21 paths — full-spectrum anime)

Covered: character moods, canon characters/combat, Japanese places, fantasy/cosmic, mecha, yokai.
Gaps: **no rain register, no train register, no winter, no vehicles/racing.** These are four of the most
iconic anime registers.

- **T1 `anime-rain`** — the Garden-of-Words register: rain-soaked shrine steps, shared umbrellas,
  puddle-reflection streets, rain on train windows, hydrangeas in June rain. Money-shot = `rain_play`
  (how the rain catches light). Character optional-small; the RAIN is the hero.
- **T1 `anime-trains`** — THE anime motif: countryside single-car trains at golden hour, level-crossing
  moments (signal lights, wind), train interiors with sunset windows, sea-side track curves
  (5cm/sec + Spirited-Away sea-train lineage). Scene-led with optional figure.
- **T2 `winter-anime`** — snow-country register: first-snow streets, snow festivals with ice lanterns,
  kotatsu-window glow from outside, shrine torii in snowfall. Completes the season coverage.
- **T2 `night-touge`** — Initial-D lineage: 90s Japanese sports cars drifting mountain passes at night,
  neon wangan highways, vending-machine glow pit stops. A subject class (cars) the bot has zero of.

## OceanBot (14 paths — naval lore × NatGeo ocean)

- **T1 `pixel-item-shop`** — the beloved genre interior: potion shop counters (Moonlighter/Recettear),

- **T1 `retro-racing`** — OutRun register: pixel sports car on a coastal highway at sunset, palm parallax,

- **T2 `pixel-overworld`** — the classic JRPG world-MAP view: top-down tile continents, tiny walled towns,
  mountain ranges, a ship sprite on tile sea, cloud shadows. Distinct from epic-vista (side-view
  parallax) — this is the nostalgic map-screen register.
